# Surface Brief — Portfolio Home (`/`)

- **Scope:** Full single-page interactive portfolio (`/`), Stage Panel drawer system, persistent Agent Dock, Voice Lab live pipeline, and LiveKit voice/text orchestration.
- **Visitor Mode:** Experience (with Persuade clarity in the first viewport).

## Direction contract

- **THESIS:** Turn the portfolio itself into a live Voice AI control surface where a real-time 3D Acoustic Iris agent speaks with visitors and drives Stage Panels, live pipeline telemetry, and a multitrack experience timeline — refusing the static developer hero + skill badges + project card grid + corner chatbot template.
- **OWN-WORLD:** **Signal Room** — deep graphite-ink ground (`#0B0D10`), anodized slate surfaces (`#12161C`), hairline acoustic grid ticks, warm broadcast white (`#F2EFE9`) and muted slate (`#8B939E`), with vermilion-amber (`#FF5A1F`) reserved strictly for active speaking/execution/playhead and phosphor mint (`#38D9A9`) for live mic input. **Archivo** tight grotesque display + **Shippori Mincho** editorial chapter titles + **JetBrains Mono** strictly for real telemetry (`ms`, `kHz`, RPC action names). Zero glassmorphism blur spam, zero purple gradients, zero progress bars.
- **STORY:** Within 5 seconds a recruiter or founder sees a real-time Voice AI engineer's instrument, clicks "Talk to my AI" (or a prompt chip / text input), watches the Acoustic Iris respond to live audio while opening OpenCalls or scrubbing the Experience tracks, verifies real RPC actions and latency in the Signal Trace, and reaches out directly.
- **FIRST VIEWPORT:** Left-aligned oversized Archivo headline (`"Engineering Intelligence Into Conversations."`) + crisp one-sentence positioning and subtle scroll anchor; right-aligned 3D WebGL Acoustic Iris Agent Core with live state readout, primary `"Talk to my AI"` trigger, secondary `"Type instead"` toggle, and three clickable starter prompt chips — framed by a sparse top broadcast bar with live connection status.
- **FORM:** Code-first build of Direction A (`Signal Room`), user-selected from `design_directions.md` over approved `wireframes.md`. `seed: none (pinned)` — "Direction A: Signal Room — Japanese broadcast studio + Evangelion-style stark editorial (graphite, vermilion-amber, phosphor mint, Archivo + Shippori Mincho, 3D Acoustic Iris)".
- **FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
